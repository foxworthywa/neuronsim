# Lab: Hormones and feedback (EndoSim Part A)

*Paste this into a Canvas assignment (a **group** assignment, submission type **File upload**). The
questions are on the group worksheet, `docs/worksheet/EndoSim-partA-worksheet.pdf` (or `.docx` to
fill in on a computer). The answer key and grading rubric are kept out of this public repository.*

---

**Time:** about 40 minutes in the simulation, plus the worksheet. Work in your lab group. One
person drives the simulation on a laptop or desktop; everyone predicts and discusses.

**Open the simulation:** [EndoSim, Part A](https://foxworthywa.github.io/neuronsim/endo.html)

### What to do

1. **Before you open the simulation**, do Part A of the worksheet together (the thyroid axis
   diagram and the receptor question).
2. Work through all eight scenes. At each scene number on the worksheet, stop and fill in that
   box together. **Write your group's prediction before you press each run button.** The
   simulation's own prediction questions are not graded, so guess honestly.
3. On the last screen, copy **your results** and type **everyone's names** (separated by commas)
   to get your group's **completion code**. Paste the results onto the worksheet (or attach
   them) and write the code in the box in scene 8.
4. Answer Part C together in full sentences, using **your own** numbers from the simulation.
   Explain *why*, not just *what*: the points are for reasoning that links cause to effect.
5. Upload one completed worksheet for the group.

### Points (40)

| Section | Points |
|---|---|
| A · Before the sim: the axis diagram; where the receptors are | 4 |
| B · During the sim: scene boxes 1–8, with your predictions and observations | 14 |
| Completion code and results pasted | 4 |
| C · After the sim: Maria's three questions, Daniel's crisis, Graves' disease | 18 |

### The patients

**Maria Okafor, 41,** is tired, cold, heavier and constipated. Her TSH is very high and her free
T4 is low: Hashimoto's thyroiditis. She starts levothyroxine. **Daniel Price, 67,** her neighbour,
stopped an 8-week course of prednisone all at once and was admitted with an adrenal crisis. Scene
8 adds two short cases, Ms. Rivera and Mr. Tran.
