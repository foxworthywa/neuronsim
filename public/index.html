How to use Muscle Explorer

  • Rotate: drag. Zoom: scroll or pinch. Pan: right-drag or two-finger drag.
  • Double-click (or double-tap) a spot to make it the centre of rotation.
  • Click a muscle to name it. Its partner on the other side lights up too, but rotation stays centred on the side you clicked.
  • Muscles from the course list are red. Other muscles are grey. You don't need to know them.
  • Deep muscles: pick one from the list and anything covering it turns see-through. You can also use Peel away, or click a covering muscle and press Hide.
  • Quiz tests you on the sections you choose: find it on the model, name it, or pick what it does.
