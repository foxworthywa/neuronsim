Source used: release 3.0 as binary STL, from https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D

Changes made (tools/build-model.mjs):
  - kept muscles, tendons and the skeleton; left out skin, organs, vessels and nerves
  - simplified every mesh (about 21 million triangles reduced to about 0.76 million)
  - removed the anterior rectus sheath: the parts of the external oblique, internal oblique and
    transversus abdominis meshes lying directly in front of rectus abdominis
  - rotated into a Y-up coordinate system in metres, and compressed (meshopt)
